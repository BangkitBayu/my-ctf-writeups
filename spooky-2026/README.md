## Overview

SpookyCTF is an annual, Halloween-themed cybersecurity competition organized in a hybrid format (both in-person and virtual) by the Cyber ​​Security Network (CSN) at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) Newark, New Jersey, United States of America.
